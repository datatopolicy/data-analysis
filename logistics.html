Logistics

Venue, schedule and travel details for Istanbul.

Venue
Sheraton Istanbul City Center — Hacıahmet Mah., Kurtuluş Deresi Cad. No:23, Beyoğlu, 34440 Istanbul, Türkiye
Dates
Monday 29 March – Friday 2 April 2027
Daily hours
09:00–17:00, with a tea break at 10:30–11:00 and lunch at 13:00–14:00
Check-in / check-out
Hotel check-in from 15:00; check-out by 12:00
Nearest airport
Istanbul Airport (IST), roughly a 35–40 minute drive. Istanbul Sabiha Gökçen (SAW) is also an option, further from the venue.
Neighborhood
Beyoğlu, within walking distance of Istiklal Avenue and about 15 minutes on foot from Taksim Square
Meeting space
The hotel has an on-site conference center with seven meeting rooms, suitable for plenary sessions and parallel country-team breakouts

Good to know

  • What to bringA laptop with Excel (2016 or later) installed and licensed, plus the priority disease/condition dataset pre-loaded per the pre-workshop deliverables.
  • Visa requirementsVary by nationality. Contact the organizing team for country-specific guidance and any invitation letter needed for the visa application.
  • Dress codeBusiness casual is the assumed default for workshop sessions; confirm if a different standard applies.
  • LanguageSessions run in English. Turkish is the local language; English is widely spoken at the hotel and in central Istanbul.
  • CurrencyTurkish lira (TRY). Major cards are widely accepted in Beyoğlu; carrying some cash is useful for smaller purchases.
